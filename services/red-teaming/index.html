Red Teaming

A goal-based simulation of a real intrusion — testing whether your people, processes, and detection tooling actually catch an attacker, not just whether a vulnerability exists.

Get a fixed-price quote Back to Offensive Security
Why it matters

Why organizations invest here

A penetration test answers "can this system be broken into?" A red team engagement answers a harder question: "would we notice, and how would we respond?" Most organizations discover the gap between those two answers during a real incident, when it's far more expensive to learn. Running the exercise on your own terms — with a defined objective and a debrief afterward — turns that gap into a fixable finding instead of a headline.

By the numbers
IBM DBIR 2025
181 days
Mean Time to Detect
Breaches now go undetected for a mean of 181 days before containment even begins, and incidents involving compromised credentials or lateral movement typically run even longer (IBM Cost of a Data Breach Report, 2025). That's the exact window a red team exercise is designed to close.
