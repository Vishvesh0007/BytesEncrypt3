Why It Matters

Why organizations invest here

Most breaches don't start with a novel zero-day exploit — they start with a flaw that careful manual testing is specifically built to catch: a broken access control, a misconfigured permission, or a business-logic shortcut a scanner can't reason about. Regular, adversary-style testing shrinks that window before it ever opens.
